Huygens N-slit interference

Secondary wavelets radiate from every point inside each slit. Their superposition produces both the single-slit diffraction envelope (sinβ/β)² and the multi-slit interference factor (sinNδ/sinδ)². Use the presets to see the transition from 1 to 10 slits.
