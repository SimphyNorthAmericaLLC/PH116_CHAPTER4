With attenuation on, each source radiates a cylindrical wave whose amplitude falls off as 1/r: E = A₀[cos(kr₁)/r₁ + cos(kr₂)/r₂], with k = 2π/λ. The color map and screen profile show |E| normalized to its maximum value over the plotted region. Because the two path amplitudes 1/r₁ and 1/r₂ are no longer equal away from the perpendicular bisector, destructive interference is no longer perfectly dark — minima become shallower (and the whole pattern fades) farther from the sources. With attenuation off, the original idealized far-field proxy |cos(π(r₂-r₁)/λ)| is shown instead, where fringe visibility is always 100%.
