Chapter knowledge check multiple choice

Two coherent sources emit waves of wavelength λ = 2 m and are placed 4 m apart. An observer is equidistant from both sources (Δr = 0). Moving along the observation line, at what path difference Δr does the first destructive minimum occur?

Δr = λ/2 = 1 m. The first destructive minimum occurs when Δr = (m + ½)λ with m = 0, giving Δr = λ/2 = 1 m. At this path difference, one wave is exactly half a cycle ahead of the other — they are perfectly out of phase (Δφ = π) and cancel. The source separation of 4 m is irrelevant to the condition for destructive interference.
