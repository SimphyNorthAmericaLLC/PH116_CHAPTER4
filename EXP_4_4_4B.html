Chapter 2-7

Single-slit diffraction — Huygens to Fraunhofer

A single opening is not a single source. By Huygens' principle, every point along the slit emits a new wavelet. Their superposition — evaluated in the far field — produces the sinc² intensity pattern that is the fingerprint of diffraction.
